Today I'm finally starting the Multi-Phase Brothel.  Well, not really starting today, but I'm starting officially today
because I'm making the website.  Much like others in this space, such as the Cat Girl Synth or Kosmo, I've decided to
name the modular synth that I'm building.  That name is Multi-Phase Brothel.

.. TEASER_END

I've been collecting parts and pieces for many years now.  Recently, for some unexplained reason and after many false
starts, I've finally put some modules together.  Most of the ones I have now, which are not many, are from Look Mum No
Computer ( https://www.lookmumnocomputer.com/projects ).  I also have a few from Cat Girl Synth, before it was taken
over by Elby Design ( https://www.elby-designs.com/webtek/cgs/cgs.htm )(it's nice to seem them continuing to carry that
torch).  I plan on making many more modules from a number of other sources, and maybe even designing a few of my own.
I have a vision, though it may never come to be, of having an entire room with racks of modules covering the walls.  A
vision of using those modules to not just make sounds and instruments, but being able to play entire songs on that
modular synth.  It's an aspirational vision that I just may achieve after I become an old man.

For the Multi-Phase Brothel, I'm starting off with Sam Battle's Kosmo Format, but with some modifications.  When I first
started collecting parts, I was planning on using the Mother of the Modular (MOTM) format.  I originally chose that
because, at the time, I thought the Cat Girl Synth used the primarily used the MOTM format.  The first couple of PCBs
for modules that I purchased were Cat Girl Synth modules from Ken Stone.  I never got around to making panels for them,
so it was easy to decide to change to the Kosmo format.  Another reason I was originally looking at the MOTM, and
another similar format, was because they're a multiple of the rack unit (MOTM is 4U tall).  I was originally planning on
using standard 19in racks, but in recent times, I've seen that those racks waste a lot of space for modular synths.
After finding the 'Look Mum No Computer' Youtube channel, and purchasing a number of Sam's Kosmo modules, I figured the
Kosmo format was a good one to use.  The biggest variance from the Kosmo format for me is the power connectors.  I much
prefer the large 4 pin Molex style connectors of the MOTM format.  I'm still using the +/- 12V power of the Kosmo and
EuroRack formats, so I've made adapters to change from one plug to the other.

Going forward, the plan for this website is to document my progress towards that far off vision of a room sized modular
song synthesizer.  I'll work on keeping it up to date, writing about interesting (to me) things, and documenting the
modules that I build and include in the 'Multi-Phase Brothel'.  I also plan on copying information about the modules I
build from the source websites, and possibly other websites.  This is to build a collection of information about the
modules.  I'd try to link to the source websites, or their Wayback Machine versions, in order to provide attribution.
